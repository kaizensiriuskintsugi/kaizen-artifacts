THE WORN TRAILS — ROLES CODEX

v0.1 · working artifact · stage: intake · built 2026-08-26 · mirrors the vault, never replaces it — nothing here is canon until reconciled

Every role is a hat: self-taken, declaring presence never mastery, shelvable, blendable — align first, blend later. Roles are the economy's verbs: each one generates, carries, or tends one of the three registers. The name never elevates; the ground remembers.

The more-hats principle (ruled 2026-08-26): more hats, with specific roles, so people can be precise about how they choose to show up. Blending is encouraged — take on many. And the smallest participation mechanisms get identity too, so people are validated at every level of their journey through this game-based expression network.

The Three Registers

Ratified 2026-08-26 · fire / light / ground holds

People carry

FIRE

Moving, carried, self-taken. The living energy — warmth, fuel, guidance, weight-sharing.

resources: warmth · cordwood · sparks (burned)

Structures stand as

LIGHT

Fixed, named positions. Gradient: flag → blaze/reblaze → light marker → lighthouse. They shine; they never chase. Powered only by fire.

resources: sparks · brightness (analog, never a number)

Practices are

GROUND

Trails worn by those who came before. Lifecycle: flag → blaze → reblaze → worn. Honoured by reblazing in-system, provenance links before-system.

resources: depth (permanent) · warmth (decays)

Fire Roles — the locked seven

From the founding constitution's vocabulary · status LOCKED 2026-08-22

Hat

locked · meta
"How I'm choosing to show up right now" — the container every role lives in. Self-written.
Mechanic: shelved, not removed; often picked back up at the request of others — the ask precedes the offer.
Declares presence, never mastery. No endorsements, ever ("12 people vouched" is a tribunal with better manners).

Torch

locked
"I'm trying to guide the way as best as I currently know how to." Heads-up mode, made visible.
Carries: guidance, on-trail, in the moment.
Logged verbatim — the three hedges ARE the guard. A torch declares walking, never validates followers.

Firekeeper

locked
Duration of tending a focus. The in-place half of the Fire Bearer / Keeper pair.
Tends: one fire, over time.
A badge, not a rank. Displayed absolutely, never comparatively.

Flagger

locked
Notices a possibility and marks the line before any tread exists. Real trail-crew term.
Generates: flags — the smallest possible lighthouse. Trials live in the unclaimed ground between selves.
A flag is noticed possibility, never an assignment or a claim.

Reblazer

locked
Walks ground someone else marked and leaves their own mark — layered, many, like glaze coats.
Generates: depth (permanent wear) + the honouring of those before.
Records the reblazer's own act — "I learned fire poi here," never "great teacher."

Witness

locked
Attests that it happened. Never how well it went. Structurally necessary — only the third position can see elevation.
Generates: occurrence records; triangulation.
First person about themselves only ("Watching that, I felt uneasy" — never "you were uneasy").

Shoulderer

locked
"I'm carrying something heavy" — and the one who helps carry. Capability arriving as a human act, not a billable one.
Moves: weight between people.
Offer, never expect — otherwise it becomes a martyrdom economy. Unnamed by default, nameable by choice.

Fire Roles — the movement expansion

Proto-roles from the 2026-08-26 session · naming in progress · all inherit hat mechanics

The Wood-Harvest Role

naming open
Opens trails through knowledge terrain and gathers the material movement yields. Trail-creation IS the wood source — the timber comes from opening the path, never from clearcutting.
Generates: cordwood (stored fuel) + new trails. Carries: that knowledge to nearby campfires.
Name candidates: Forager · Gatherer (both sustainable-harvest coherent — taking what regrows) · Trailwright (wright = maker; craft register) · "Hunter" needs care (predation register in a consent-built network — deliberate read required).
Refusals bind: never Explorer / Scout / Pioneer / Frontier (doctrine of discovery) · never Trailblazer / Trailhead / Blazer (Salesforce).
Direction per the more-hats principle: Forager and Gatherer likely SEPARATE hats — precision over consolidation. Expectations pass will settle the boundary.

Hunter

HALTED — contradiction at focus
Kai-redefined twice on 2026-08-26: first as the ecosystem's immune system (hunts the toxic components that over-harm and over-replicate — tracks the sleepy toxin, never the sleeper), then adjusted toward "enforcer" — behaviour adjustments or "elimination" of problematic members or data flagged by enough concerned parties.
The collision (why this card is halted): the founding constitution forbids the enforcer shape three ways — right and wrong are self-expressed only (rule 2) · "flagged by enough parties" is a count-threshold verdict, and sorted counts are verdicts (rule 9) · "12 people vouched" was refused as a tribunal with better manners, and 12 people flagging is the same tribunal inverted (rule 29). An enforcement hat also can't be self-taken, and conferral was refused as certification.
What canon already requires: founding Q7 (launch dependency) demands a real removal path — human review, failure clause, consent policy. Moderation is required, not forbidden. The open question is its SHAPE.
Reconciling shape on the table: flags raise an issue to FOCUS (attention, never verdict) → a consented human-review PROCESS judges (the Q7 removal path; possibly seated with the External Witness) → the Hunter stays the pattern-tracker who brings toxins to focus — the immune system's detector, never its executioner.
HALTED for Kaizen's ruling: tracker-to-focus with enforcement as a separate consented process — or supersede the no-verdict rules themselves.

The Discovery Role

naming open
The Hunter / Gatherer / Forager equivalent for discovering new territory, knowledge, connections, resources, and opportunities — the one who goes out past the worn ground.
Generates: flags on unclaimed terrain, new connections, found resources and opportunities, brought back to the fires.
Name candidates: FARWALKER (lead — one who walks further out and brings back what's there; non-claiming, fresh coinage, and the Vision seat already says it: "stories back from further than we could go alone") · Seeker (playful, game register) · Wayfarer (traveler; sits near LBP's Wayfinder line — collision watch).
The refusals table hits hardest here: Explorer, Discoverer, Scout, Pioneer, Frontier, Settler are all refused. Discovery must be named as movement and return — never as claiming.
Open: does Farwalker ring true?

Walker

ratified 08-26
The base hat — everyone's first, worn the moment you're on the trails at all. "I am walking." Presence validated before any contribution exists.
Generates: warmth wherever they pass; the beginning of wear.
Serves the validation-at-all-levels ruling: nobody on the network is roleless. Kaizen: "perfect example roles for how I want to see this unfolding."

Stoker

ratified 08-26
One who adds fuel to fires they stand by — the smallest support mechanism, given an identity. Standing-by made visible as a hat.
Moves: cordwood into other people's stockpiles; their own recorded act, never an endorsement.
Supporting others is a role, not an absence of one.

Trail Re-former

Kai-proposed · naming open
Takes the line a Flagger noticed and forms it into ground others can walk — "explores the raw possibilities of the natural experience and begins reforming it into what others can explore as well."
Generates: walkable trail from flagged raw terrain (and the timber that opening yields). Chain: Flagger notices → Re-former forms → walkers wear.
Name candidates: Re-former (Kai; the hyphen carries the FORM sense) · Trailwright (wright = maker) · Trailshaper. Constraint (Kai): no "clear" and no full/terminating language — forming, never clearing.
Open: pick the name; boundary with Forager/Gatherer settles in the expectations pass.

Fire Bearer

Kai-proposed
Carries fire between camps — explores fire knowledge at different locations and finds the new paths that connect it.
Moves: embers and fuel fire-to-fire; connection itself. Likely absorbs the "fire-connector" proto-role.
Bearing is movement, keeping is duration — a function pair, never a rank pair.
Open: is Fire Bearer the ratified name for the connector role?

Light Bearer

Kai-proposed
Carries light along the trails — the moving half of the light pair.
Moves: markers into place? sparks to structures? — the nuance is genuinely open.
Same pair logic: function, not rank.
Open: what exactly does a Light Bearer carry that a Fire Bearer doesn't?

Light Keeper

Kai-proposed
Tends light structures in place — keeps a marker or lighthouse cared for and burning true.
Tends: structures; possibly the generator itself on the operator's behalf, when invited.
The operator still owns the generator — a keeper tends by consent, never by claim.
Open: overlap with Path Tender — same role at different scales, or two roles?

Path Tender

Kai-named
Keeps markers and lights cared for along the trails — stewardship of the walked world.
Tends: the trail-level layer: markers, wayposts, small lights. Prevents quiet decay.
Tending is recorded as the tender's own act; care is never a claim on the thing cared for.
Open: boundary with Light Keeper (structure-scale vs trail-scale?).

The Bearer / Keeper Pattern

Bearing moves. Keeping tends. Every register can have both: a bearer carries it between places (movement, connection, circulation) and a keeper sustains it in place (duration, care, cadence). Fire Bearer / Fire Keeper · Light Bearer / Light Keeper — and the ground register already has its pair in the Reblazer (who moves wear along trails) and the Path Tender (who keeps them).

The pattern is horizontal, not vertical: a keeper is not a promoted bearer. Duration and movement are different gifts, honored differently — the keeper's tending badge and the bearer's carried-ember credits are both absolute displays, never compared.

On Hierarchy — the question, answered by the constitution

Q (Kaizen, 2026-08-26): do we consider hierarchy of hat naming to honor the time, effort, and proof of understanding an individual has in an area of knowledge?

What the founding constitution says: no. Three locks close the door on name-rank: badges are never ranks · hats declare presence, never mastery · conferral reintroduces certification — and "proof of understanding" is a certification claim wearing trail clothes. A naming ladder would also become the vulnerability ladder the openness rule forbids.

What already honors time and effort, without rank: depth — total wear, permanent, never decays · firekeeper-style duration badges — displayed absolutely, never comparatively · first-blaze origin credit — permanent, by name · ember credit — knowledge carried always names its source · and now light: a structure others keep fueling is visibly, analogically bright.

The name never elevates; the ground remembers. "A trail is the only thing that can't lie about how trusted it is."

Status: session position, pending Kaizen's ring-true. If it holds, hat names stay flat forever and honor lives entirely in wear, duration, credit, and light.

Resource Flow — how the roles power the world

The loop is anti-extractive by shape: light attracts walkers → walking deepens trails and generates wood → wood feeds fires → fires make sparks → sparks make light. Nothing in the loop can be bought yet; when purchase unparks, revenue runs artist support programs. Self-fuel keeps a light LIT; amplification outward always comes from others.
