Pairing — serif display over Inter body

A practice, not a product

Fraunces carries warmth and a made-by-hand irregularity — the soft and wonk axes give it a slight lean that reads as crafted rather than classical. Inter underneath is neutral and modern, so the serif keeps all of the personality and none of the argument. At UI sizes Inter stays perfectly legible on warm charcoal, which is the pairing's real job.

What Fraunces gives: warmth, craft, a contemporary voice with a deliberate quirk. It is sturdy on dark — strokes hold at weight 400 and get authoritative at 600. Variable axes (opsz, soft, wonk) mean one file tunes from hero line to card title.
What it costs: the wonk is a personality that not every surface wants; it reads modern-crafted rather than literary-classical. On grant and institutional surfaces it may feel less formal than KSK sometimes needs.
